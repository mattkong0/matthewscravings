KOREA




Jeju Olae Noodles

Located in Ilsan, just west of Seoul, this restaurant has been visited by many celebrities and is known for their Meat Noodle Soup, Jeju's signature dish.



  • Affordable price point

  • Fast service

  • Personally recommend trying their signature dish, Meat Noodle Soup











Pohang Pork Soup and Rice

Located at the "Pork and Rice Alley" (aka Seomyeon) in Busan, this one of the many restaurants in this alley that serves Busan's signature dish, 되지국밥, or pork soup and rice.



  • Fast service

  • Banchan (side dishes) are self-serve

  • Personally recomment trying their signature dish, pork soup and rice, or their boiled pork.





Gajok Hoegwan (or Family Hall)

Jeonju is known as the origin spot of Bibimbap and this restaurant maintains their style of bibimbap by adding all of the banchan (side dishes) into the bowl.



  • The portions are quite big and there are a lot of banchan (side dishes)

  • The service is foreigner-friendly

  • Personally recommend trying either their jeonju-style bibimbap (I got beef tartare) or their tteokgalbi (grilled short rib patties)
